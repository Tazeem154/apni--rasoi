About APNI RASOI

Founded in 2014, APNI RASOI has been serving the people of Jammu with authentic Indian cuisine for over a decade. What started as a small family kitchen has grown into one of the most beloved restaurants in the city, known for our commitment to quality, tradition, and exceptional hospitality.

Our chefs bring decades of experience in North Indian, Mughlai, and Tandoori cooking, using time-honored recipes passed down through generations. We source the freshest ingredients daily and prepare every dish with the same care and attention that we would for our own family.
